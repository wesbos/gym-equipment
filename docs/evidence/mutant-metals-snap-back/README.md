The September 24 revisions use user-supplied owner photos and close-ups to correct these details:

- The hanging arm consists of two straight square tubes with one mitered weld, replacing the curved open channel. The [blue arm reference](reference-arm-angle.png) establishes a visible elbow: the upper section leans farther out, with an estimated 17.6° change of direction at the weld. The estimated 2 in square section has four walls and a hollow bore. The [black/red mounted reference](reference-front-uhmw.png) locates the UHMW and four recessed screws on the front face, following both straight sections; both sides remain entirely in the selected arm steel color.
- A steel nose plate closes the full front of the housing.
- The two MM through-cuts have the staggered legs visible in the white close-up: a long left leg on the upper stroke and a long right leg on the lower stroke. The additional [blue logo reference](reference-parallel-logo.png) corrects the tapered diagonals: both marks now share 45° edges and a constant 12 mm stroke width, measured perpendicular to each edge, so the strokes and their separating gap remain parallel. Each side plate has independently oriented through-cuts, keeping the upper left leg and lower right leg correctly handed when viewed from either outside face.
- The mounting clasp has one side strap and a rear return, forming an L with the opposite side open for installation around the upright. Paired units have opposite-handed clasps; the logo-bearing side plates keep the same correctly reading cuts on each unit. The clasp and its rear protection pad sit 44 mm higher, directly below the logo rather than near the foot of the rear leg.
- The [white catch](reference-flat-foot.png) shows the bracket welded flat to the square tube's end. The foot now follows that end plane, without the old rear wall projecting up beside the arm. The front bend follows the [blue close-up](reference-lip-wrap.png), and the user's correction confirms a continuous steel plate beneath the roller with no cutout. The lip cap covers its inner and outer faces and wraps over the top and both side edges, with clearance for the roller and axle.

The current images render the actual catalog worker meshes with the app's studio lighting and creased normals. Both outside logo orientations and the front closure were visually checked with the part viewer’s shadow settings. The standard part viewer was also checked in perspective, side and front views.

Published dimensions retained: 3/8 in steel and 2-1/4 in usable roller length. Photo estimates include the 330 × 280 mm frame, 2 in tube with 1/8 in walls, approximately 17.6° arm joint, 1/4 in catch bracket, clasp height and 42 mm roller diameter. The catch inclination is derived from the lower tube (approximately −36°), keeping its base flush with the square-cut end; its roller centre is approximately 117 mm out and 193 mm below the mounting pin. The bar support and collision bounds follow the revised catch. Small welds and hardware are simplified. This is a visual reconstruction, not fabrication CAD.
