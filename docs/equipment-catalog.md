# Most common home-gym equipment

Research list of the ~265 most-owned pieces of home/garage gym equipment, for deciding what to model next.

**Source:** [gymradar.com/equipment](https://gymradar.com/equipment), pulled 2026-09-19. Each category page is sorted by "Most Owned", meaning the number of user-submitted gyms that list the item. **Gyms** below is that count. I took the top of every category (and page 2 for rack attachments), and dropped merch and non-equipment items (flags, drink holders, the Gym Radar "certified facility" package, coasters, bar brushes).

The counts show how popular an item is among Gym Radar users. That audience leans heavily American garage gym (REP and Rogue dominate), so treat the numbers as relative, not absolute.

**In app:** ✅ = this exact product is modeled · ≈ = a generic equivalent is modeled (for example the 20 kg Olympic barbell stands in for most power bars)

## Biggest gaps, by ownership

These are the most-owned items that have no equivalent in the app yet, and fit the rack-builder's current placement modes:

- **Rack attachments:** belt squat attachment (Mammoth, 164), rack rollers and leg rollers (~50–70 each), seat/pad attachments (Pegasus, Pritchett, Thresher, seal row pad), band pegs, landmine handles, flip-down safeties, lever arms
- **Floor items:** specialty bars (SSB 162/127/108, trap bar 134, curl bar 112, axle 89), flat/competition benches (FB-5000 114, Thompson Fat Pad), change plates, fixed hex dumbbells and kettlebells, reverse hyper and GHD, Echo Bike and RowErg (193/172)
- **Wall and storage:** vertical plate tree, gun rack and vertical bar holders, band/belt hanger, weight horns
- **Cable attachments:** V-grip (234), long ab strap (167), Eclipse grip (158), Angles 90 (145), TrakHandle (142), Darko Shorty/Longy bars and Danglers
