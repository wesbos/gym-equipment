## Mounting

Source origin is the rack-hole axis at tube center. +Y points along the handle root and mounting pin; +Z is the free grip. The left version has its closed web at +X; the right version mirrors it to −X. Mounted on opposite inward-facing upright faces, both grips point up and both closed webs face forward, matching the squat example.

On crossmembers the bracket rotates ±90° about its pin axis depending on hand. Each web rests on top of the rail; both root grips project out from its side, while the free grips run along the rail in opposite directions. The pin remains coaxial with the actual side bore. Two handles use distinct stations on the same rail (suggested spacing 450 mm, adjustable by selecting the second station). Existing spanning rail attachments retain their parallel-rail pair behavior.

The 75 mm project rack and nominal 76.2 mm tubing use the same published liner and steel thicknesses; only the fitted cavity follows the rack. Smaller/rectangular tubes and 5/8 in holes are refused. Splitting a pair persists the opposite hand on the second unit, preserving its geometry and rail orientation.

Validation covers published grip dimensions, valid solids, paired/split hands, save/load, rail station moves, incompatible racks, and boolean intersection of the actual handle/pin meshes against generated rack members on both 75 mm and true 3-inch racks.
