# Specialty cable grips (issue #126)

Sources checked 2026-09-19. Popularity is from Gym Radar (Sep 2026). Every entry hangs from one pegboard hook with the same
carabiner-anchor contract as the REP v1 set (`parts/cable-attachments.ts`): origin on the hook rod axis where the eye, ring, strap or
hook seat rests on the rod; X along the wall, −Y out of it, Z up; `hook: 1` adds the J-peg. Envelopes are the build bounds rounded
up (the family test checks containment and tightness).

Hang parts carry no product params (the hang registry only persists `{panel, slot}`), so real size variants ship as separate
entries (CarbonFlex 24″/48″) or as the default size (KAZ small, ARC 5° frame with 8″ handles), and each entry has one fixed hero
colour. Pairs hang together on one hook when both fit on the peg (A90, Danglers, KAZ, Swissies); bulky pairs show one handle
(TrakHandle, RO-T8, KORIKAHM), like the Rogue ankle cuff in `cable-handles`. The two Kleva Atlas pieces are deeper than the peg
reach, so they slide back along the rod until their rear face is at the panel face (y ≤ 75 mm).

Photos were downloaded to the agent scratchpad (Shopify `products/<handle>.json`, Squarespace `?format=json`, BigCommerce and
Wix product pages, Rogue's asset CDN and Amazon listings) and compared side by side with front, iso and side renders in two
review rounds. Where a vendor publishes fewer than eight photos, all of them were used and sister listings that show the same
part were added (noted per product).
