## Published dimensions used

All lengths are the manufacturer's inch figures converted at 25.4 mm/in. **Inside** is the distance between the inner collar faces. The collar (shoulder) length is always derived as (overall − inside − 2 × loadable sleeve) / 2, so the build hits the published overall length, collar span and sleeve length exactly.

## Measured from photos (estimates)

- **Knurl extents.** The Rogue dimension graphic gives the OPB a 16.9375" smooth centre and the Deadlift Bar 17". Scaling the same graphic puts the OPB centre knurl at about 4.7", so the model uses 120 mm. The grip knurl stops about 12 mm before a Rogue collar. On Texas bars it stops about 14 mm short, because the Texas collar close-ups show a short smooth run-out ahead of the bronze thrust washer. REP bars leave about 30 mm. Multipurpose and REP bars start the grip knurl 215 mm from centre, like the Olympic bar.
- **Collars.** Rogue shoulders are thin bright flanges about 58 mm across, with the bronze bushing ring showing at the shaft. Texas uses thick classic chrome cylinders about 63 mm across (60 mm on the PRO's thinner collar, 66 mm on the Squat Bar), with a bronze thrust washer. REP uses thin rounded flanges about 60 mm across in the sleeve finish, laser-etched, with no bronze showing. The American Barbell shoulder carries the patented recessed TIG-weld groove, modelled as a rough bead in a groove at the sleeve root.
- **End caps.** Rogue caps sit about 1.2 mm deep behind an internal snap ring. Rogue OPB: black cap, white centre block and ring text. Ohio Bar: black with a red disc. Deadlift: black with a gold centre. Bella: grey. Boneyard: black with white crossed bones. Texas caps sit about 7 mm deep inside a bronze bushing ring: black, with a white ring of text and a white centre mark. REP Colorado: navy badge with a red sky, a yellow sun and white peaks. Black Diamond: black cap with a white diamond outline. Double Black Diamond: silver cap with a double diamond. Chewy: black cap with a grey starburst. All emblems are plain geometric plates, never copied logo artwork.
- **Knurl relief.** Real knurl pitch is 1–2.5 mm (20 TPI on the Chewy), too fine to render at bar scale. The model draws a diamond grid of pyramids, the intersection of a ring-ribbed lathe and a fluted prism, at a 4.4–5 mm visual pitch. Relief depth follows the grade: Double Black Diamond and Texas deepest, the Bella and Colorado shallow, the Texas PRO "Medium" 40% shallower than "Aggressive". Knurl on dark coatings renders lighter than the coating, because the cut points catch light. On bright metal it renders slightly darker.
- **Finishes** (colour, metalness, roughness) come from the photos. Bright zinc is bluish-bright, black zinc a dark satin, and stainless a warm grey. Cerakote is matte colour with low metalness. Duracoat and Rogue's "proprietary matte black" sleeves are near-black satin.

## Bar geometry contract (cradles and plate stacks)

A parking floor part may declare `bar: BarSpec | (params) => BarSpec` (`floor-part.ts`) with its shaft diameter, half grip span (`shaftHalf`), sleeve start, sleeve length, sleeve diameter and floor axis height. `barSpec(part, params)` in `barbell-cradles.ts` resolves it. Parts without a spec get `DEFAULT_BAR_SPEC`, the 20 kg Olympic bar (`floor-parts/barbell.ts`), so `olympic-barbell` and any bar that only sets `parks: true` behave exactly as before.

- `barCradles(resolved, bar?)` and `freeCradles(resolved, items, movingId, bar?)` accept a support span only if it fits between that bar's collars: 300 mm ≤ span ≤ 2·shaftHalf − 40 mm. `freeCradles` defaults to the moving item's own spec.
- `parkedPose(cradle, bar?)` places the floor-item origin so the bar's shaft bottom sits where the 28.5 mm reference shaft's does. The cradle rest points are 28.5 mm shaft axes, so the axis moves by (shaft − 28.5)/2. A 31.75 mm Texas Squat Bar rides 1.6 mm higher, a 25 mm Bella 1.75 mm lower.
- `parkBarbells` and `settleBarbells` resolve every parked item with its own spec: a bar whose collars cannot straddle its cradle drops to the floor.
- `barSleeves(bar, spec?)` returns each sleeve's world origin (inner collar face), axis, loadable length and diameter for plate stacks.

The bars build with local X along the bar and the origin on the floor under the centre. The axis sits at collar radius, so the collars rest on the floor. The footprint is overall length × collar diameter. Scenery only, excluded from print export.
