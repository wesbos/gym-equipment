# Staple cable handles, bars, straps and hardware (issue #125)

Sources checked 2026-09-19. Popularity is from Gym Radar (Sep 2026). All entries hang from one pegboard hook with the same
carabiner-anchor contract as the REP v1 set (`parts/cable-attachments.ts`): origin on the hook rod axis where the eye, ring or
bail rests on the rod; X along the wall, −Y out of it, Z up; `hook: 1` adds the J-peg. Envelopes are the build bounds rounded up
(the family test checks both containment and tightness).

Hang parts carry no product params (the hang registry only persists `{panel, slot}`), so real size variants that change the
envelope ship as separate entries (Rogue straight lat bar 20″/40″, the eight BLUSLM pieces). Colourways are fixed to the
default/most-photographed one noted below.

Webbing products (single handle, Spud ab strap, daisy chains, tricep straps, ankle-cuff heel strap) are modelled **flat-laid**:
the strap face points out of the wall, the way they appear in product photos and on a pegboard. Real webbing twists; that
twist is not modelled.

Photos were downloaded to the agent scratchpad and compared side by side with renders (front and iso views, two review rounds).
