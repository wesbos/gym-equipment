# Mats, platforms, jacks & floor accessories (issue #124) reconstruction notes

Sources checked 2026-09-19. For every product, photos were downloaded (8–25 each: manufacturer galleries via Shopify `products.json` or the Rogue asset CDN, Amazon hi-res images, Gym Radar owner photos) and reviewed as contact sheets plus close-ups. Popularity is from Gym Radar (`/equipment/<slug>` "Owned by" counts), and the catalog lists entries highest-owned first. The published size tables live in `floor-parts/floor-accessories.ts`; the builders in `parts/floor-accessories-{mats,jacks,soft,voltra}.ts` share `parts/floor-accessories-kit.ts`.

## Placement: mats and platforms are underlays

Stall mats, the DIY platform and the Rogue platform set `underlay: true` (a new optional `FloorPartSpec` flag). `floorWarnings` skips overlap and clearance checks against underlays, and `addFloorItem` neither steps an underlay clear of other items nor steps other items clear of an underlay, so a rack, bench or jack can stand on a mat without warnings. Nothing changes for entries that don't set the flag. Items standing on a mat still sit at floor level (they sink ¾" into it visually).

## Materials and colours

Rubber, foam and vinyl are `liner`; printed/stencilled marks, steel and wood are `source` (fixed factory colours, never rack paint); zinc and black hardware are `fastener` with an authored finish. Colours are sampled from the studio photos (e.g. TSC rubber `#212223`, REP cork `#c8955f`, Echo red `#cc2a25`, GRID orange `#f08a1c`).

## Branding

No logo artwork: wordmarks, brand badges and stencils are plain flat plates in the photographed colour. Weight and angle numerals use a plain stroke font (digits, KG/LB/ESB, `-`, `.`, `°`).
